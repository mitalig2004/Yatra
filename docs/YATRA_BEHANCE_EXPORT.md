# YATRA Behance Export Guide

The canonical portfolio narrative lives at `/work/yatra`. The legacy `/case-study` URL redirects there.

## Static sequence

The page is ordered so a static full-page capture communicates the complete story without scroll animation or interaction:

1. Outcome and project metadata
2. Product premise, scope, and evidence status
3. Problem model and pain-point implications
4. Honest discovery framing and current category scan
5. Information architecture, signature flow, journey hypothesis, and reconstructed layout exploration
6. Four key UX decisions with evidence and limitations
7. Final product across entry, planning, adaptive editing, collaboration foundation, mobile, and budget
8. Internal refinement comparison
9. Visual system and accessibility commitments
10. Validation record, limitations, learning, and next research steps

Print styles remove sticky navigation and motion-dependent transforms. Reduced-motion rendering also exposes every section in its final readable state. The automated case-study check creates a paginated, print-ready static PDF and representative 390px and 1440px captures in `test-results/` for review.

## Evidence labels

- **Built** means the behavior is present in the functional local prototype.
- **Simulated** means deterministic fixture behavior stands in for a live service.
- **Future** means the concept is intentionally outside the implemented scope.
- **Reconstructed** identifies design history recreated from the final architecture because original artifacts were not retained.

No participant count, research finding, conversion result, retention result, or commercial impact is claimed without source evidence.
