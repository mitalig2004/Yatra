## Hiring-manager assessment

- **Product and contribution:** The opening identifies YATRA, Mitali Giri's product/UX/frontend role, the self-directed timeline, scope, status and tools without delaying the product reveal.
- **Problem significance:** The fragmented-planning visual connects saved inspiration to timing, route and budget consequences. The language remains a hypothesis because primary research is not documented.
- **Process legibility:** Discovery, category context, architecture, reconstructed alternatives, decisions and refinement appear in causal order.
- **Decision quality:** Proposal versus accepted state, numerical conflict explanation, synchronized revisions and the non-drag path are tied to authentic behavior.
- **Collaboration credibility:** The current single-device foundation and future identity/sync layer are visually distinct. No invitation or real-time behavior is claimed as implemented.
- **System coherence:** Editorial serif use is selective; functional UI uses Manrope. Warm neutrals, forest actions and warning amber retain their semantic roles.
- **Responsive story:** Wide diagrams collapse into vertical sequences and the case study contains no pinned horizontal mobile sequence.
- **Evidence integrity:** Reconstructed work, simulations, future concepts and unproven outcomes are explicitly labeled.
- **Memorability:** The travel-journal exploration, route animation, passport stamp, product mockups and dark signature moment provide visual cadence without replacing the UX argument.

## Remaining research risk

The portfolio can demonstrate implementation quality and product reasoning, but it cannot yet demonstrate user-validated desirability or measurable planning improvement. The next credible step is formative testing of preview comprehension, the ₹400 transport explanation, conflict resolution, Apply confidence, and Undo discovery.
